“What Do You See When You Land?” – Context Transfer from Email Marketing to the Online Store

Sen, Srijan*; Mojumder, Probal; Mukhopadhyay, Sabyasachi

India Management Research Conference (IMRC 2026), IIM Ahmedabad

This study investigates how landing page specificity impacts e-commerce conversions following email campaigns. Analyzing an electronics retailer's browsing data, findings reveal that context transfer—aligning destination page products exactly with the email creative—consistently amplifies add-to-cart, checkout, and purchase rates. Marketers must therefore actively sustain pre-click product cues within the post-click environment.
