Working Paper

Alleviating Resource Constraints in the Rural Education Sector of India: Local LLM-based Answer Generation and Evaluation

Kumar, Harsh; Mojumder, Probal; Sen, Srijan

Poor teacher-to-student ratio in rural India is a grave concern. This affects the underprivileged rural students as they can not procure enough study materials. We propose a solution to this by providing answers generated by LLMs catering to their exact needs. This study aims to investigate the optimal condition of LLMs and the associated cost for implementing the system.

Working Paper

AI Search Leads to “Kakistocracy” – How Search-as-Thinking is Reshaped by GenAI

Sen, Srijan; Bhattacharyya, Samadrita

Generative AI’s instant answers are replacing traditional search, maximizing efficiency but eliminating productive cognitive friction. Our research introduces "search-as-thinking" to highlight what is lost: active information synthesis. By encouraging passive cognitive offloading rather than mental schema development, GenAI creates an "illusion of mastery" that threatens independent reasoning and creative problem-solving.

Working Paper

“What Do You See When You Land?” – Context Transfer from Email Marketing to the Online Store

Sen, Srijan; Mojumder, Probal; Mukhopadhyay, Sabyasachi

This study investigates how landing page specificity impacts e-commerce conversions following email campaigns. Analyzing an electronics retailer's browsing data, findings reveal that context transfer—aligning destination page products exactly with the email creative—consistently amplifies add-to-cart, checkout, and purchase rates. Marketers must therefore actively sustain pre-click product cues within the post-click environment.
