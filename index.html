Srijan Sen

Research Associate, Information Systems Area, IIM Udaipur

Srijan is currently a Research Associate in the Information Systems Area at the Indian Institute of Management Udaipur. He holds a Master's in Data Science (M.Sc.) and a Bachelor's in Statistics (B.Sc.), both from St. Xavier's College, Kolkata, and was awarded the DST INSPIRE Scholarship for Higher Education (SHE) from 2018 to 2021.

An early-career academic practitioner, Srijan is seeking a Ph.D. in the Information Systems track. His research spans user behaviour on digital platforms and the broader digital transformation of organizations and society, with a particular focus on how artificial intelligence can be harnessed to benefit underprivileged communities while remaining attentive to its potential harms.

Srijan has previously served as a Project Linked Personnel at the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, where he gained experience in advanced statistical methodology, and as a Data Analyst at Nestasia, a D2C start-up, where he developed a practical understanding of business.
